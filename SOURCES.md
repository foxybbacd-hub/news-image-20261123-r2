### 2. Democrats clinch 258 House seats — Washington, USA
- The Associated Press called North Carolina's 5th district for Democrat Chuck
  Hubbard over Republican Rep. Virginia Foxx on Sunday 22 Nov (just before
  1 p.m. ET), giving Democrats at least 258 seats — the largest House majority
  won by either party in a general election since 1992, a net gain of 44 seats.
  Four races remained uncalled (Minnesota, Tennessee, two Louisiana runoffs).
- Associated Press, 22 Nov 2026 (syndicated, e.g.):

  coverage worldwide and is carried here as Monday's lead US story.

  The story led Monday 23 Nov world-news roundups.
- Reuters, 22 Nov 2026 (syndicated, e.g.):
